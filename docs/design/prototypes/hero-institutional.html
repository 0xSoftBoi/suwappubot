Custody

Keys, sealed

Wallet keys are envelope-encrypted under a KMS-held master key. Legacy material is migrated on first use. No employee can read a key.

We are not a licensed custodian. Institutions may bring their own.

Execution

Best available output

Each order is quoted across every venue live on its chain. The highest net output is selected; the policy is published.

Venue coverage is chain-gated. No single order reaches all 21.

Reporting

A receipt for every fill

Every settlement links to the transaction on the block explorer, with price impact, network cost and fee itemised.

Reports are exportable; audit access on request.

Compliance

Plain statements

We publish what we are, what we are not, and how the system is built. Certifications appear here when held, not before.
