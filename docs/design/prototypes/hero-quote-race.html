Trust, stated plainly

We would rather show you the mechanism than a badge we do not hold.

Custody

Your keys, sealed.

Wallet keys are envelope-encrypted with a KMS-held master key (kms_aesgcm_v2). Legacy material migrates on first touch. Nobody at Suwappu can read a key.

Routing

Best output wins.

Every fill is a race across the venues live on that chain. The fee and price impact are on the confirmation, not in the fine print.

Verification

Every fill has a receipt.

Each settled route links to the transaction on the block explorer. If you cannot verify it, we did not do it.
