# Distribution

Gekko's unfair advantage is that it doesn't need to build distribution from zero — Suwappu already has an active Telegram/WhatsApp trading user base and channels. The job here is choosing which distribution motions to invest in first (owned/organic, near-zero CAC) versus which to defer (paid, enterprise sales) until the crypto-native beachhead is proven out, per `NEOBANK_ROADMAP.md`'s framing that trading+banking and WhatsApp DeFi are both uncontested.

## Founder Brand

### What it is
Distribution built on the credibility and reach of a visible founder or small set of team members — their personal following, credibility in the space, and willingness to be the public face of the product.

### Playbook / options
- Founder posts build-in-public updates, transparent metrics (TVL, volume, yield paid out), and product rationale on X — this is standard in crypto GTM and converts disproportionately well vs. corporate brand accounts.
- Founder does podcast circuit on crypto/DeFi shows (Bankless, Unchained, etc. — verify current relevant shows) once there's a real launch story.
- Consistency matters more than frequency: 2-3x/week beats daily filler.

### Gekko default
Have a named founder or 2-3 core team members post regularly about Gekko's build and the "trading + banking" thesis directly, reusing Suwappu's existing credibility in the trading community rather than starting a brand-new voice from scratch.

### Target metric
- Founder account follower growth attributable to Gekko content: track monthly, no hard target until baseline established.
- % of new signups citing founder content in an onboarding survey field: target 10%+ (verify measurement method).

## Organic Content

### What it is
Non-paid content — threads, explainers, comparison posts, memes, short-form video — that drives awareness and signups without ad spend, compounding over time via search and social sharing.

### Playbook / options
- "Idle balance earns 0%" comparison content (Gekko yield vs. CEX/wallet 0%) — directly reuses the roadmap's core wedge.
- Weekly "bank statement" style posts showing aggregate yield paid to users (social proof, transparency).
- Educational content bridging trading audience to banking concepts (what is non-custodial, why does it matter).
- Repurpose one long-form piece into 5-10 short clips/threads (standard content multiplication playbook).

### Gekko default
Center organic content on the trading-to-banking bridge narrative specifically for the existing Suwappu audience — "you already trade here, now stop leaving yield on the table" — before broadening to general crypto content.

### Target metric
- Organic content → Gekko landing page click-through rate: target 2-3% (verify against actual channel benchmarks).
- Monthly organic-attributed signups as % of total signups: target 40%+ in first 2 quarters (low-paid-spend phase).

## Referral Programs

### What it is
Incentivized peer-to-peer distribution where existing users are rewarded (cash, yield boost, fee discount) for bringing in new users — historically one of the strongest channels for neobanks (Chime, Cash App, Revolut all scaled heavily on referral).

### Playbook / options
- Two-sided reward: referrer gets a yield boost or bonus, referee gets a signup bonus or fee waiver — classic Chime/Cash App structure.
- Because Gekko starts inside Telegram/WhatsApp groups, referral can be as simple as a shareable deep link that drops the referee straight into a pre-filled onboarding flow.
- Cap or taper rewards to avoid fraud rings (typical failure mode of poorly-designed referral programs) — require a funded/verified account before payout, not just signup.
- Benchmark: well-run fintech referral programs report 15-30% of new users from referral at maturity (verify against current public data).

### Gekko default
Launch referral inside the existing Suwappu Telegram bot and WhatsApp flow on day one — a trader referring another trader is the lowest-friction, highest-trust distribution loop available, and it's already sitting inside owned channels.

### Target metric
- Referral-attributed signups as % of total: target 15%+ by end of quarter 2.
- Viral coefficient (invites sent per active user × conversion rate): track monthly, target >0.15 initially.

## Strategic Partnerships

### What it is
Distribution through other companies' existing user bases — protocol partnerships, wallet integrations, exchange listings, or co-marketing deals that put Gekko in front of audiences it doesn't already own.

### Playbook / options
- Integrate with DeFi protocols already used by the target ICP (Aave, Morpho, Safe) for co-marketing or referral-style traffic.
- DAO tooling partnerships (treasury/payroll vendors) for the vertical-specific SMB motion.
- Wallet/infra partnerships (e.g., a listing or integration in a popular non-custodial wallet) for awareness (verify which wallets are open to this).
- Avoid over-indexing on partnerships requiring heavy BD cycles before product-market fit is proven.

### Gekko default
Prioritize partnerships that extend the existing crypto-native funnel (DeFi protocols, DAO tooling, Safe ecosystem) over broad co-marketing deals with unrelated consumer brands — the ROI is much higher when the partner's audience already overlaps with Gekko's ICP.

### Target metric
- Number of live distribution partnerships signed per quarter: target 2-3 meaningful ones (not just logo swaps).
- Partner-attributed signups as % of total: track once first partnership live, no target until baseline.

## Vertical SaaS

### What it is
Distribution through embedding or bundling with vertical-specific software that Gekko's target businesses already use — e.g., DAO tooling, on-chain payroll/invoicing platforms, Web3 studio management tools.

### Playbook / options
- Identify the 3-5 tools crypto-native SMBs/DAOs already run on (multisig UIs, contributor payment tools, treasury dashboards) and pursue embedded banking or referral integrations with them.
- Embedded finance pattern: Gekko becomes the banking layer under someone else's software rather than a standalone destination — higher LTV, lower CAC, but slower to negotiate.
- This is a later-stage play; requires Gekko to have a stable API/SDK for partners to build against.

### Gekko default
Defer active vertical SaaS partnerships until the SMB/DAO-treasury vertical (see `08-icp-positioning.md`) has organic traction — then pursue 1-2 embedded integrations with DAO tooling vendors rather than building a broad partner program prematurely.

### Target metric
- Not tracked in year one; revisit once 10+ DAO/protocol treasury accounts are active (see ICP doc target).

## Enterprise Sales

### What it is
A dedicated outbound sales motion — SDRs, AEs, demos, procurement cycles — targeting larger accounts (middle market and up) that require high-touch relationship selling rather than self-serve signup.

### Playbook / options
- Standard B2B SaaS/fintech sales motion: outbound prospecting, demo, pilot, procurement/compliance review, contract.
- Requires dedicated headcount and sales collateral distinct from the self-serve consumer/SMB funnel.
- Long sales cycles (quarters) are normal for bank-adjacent products, especially with compliance review.

### Gekko default
No enterprise sales motion in year one. Gekko is self-serve-only through Telegram/WhatsApp/app for the crypto-native consumer and SMB beachhead; building an enterprise sales team now would misallocate scarce resources against a segment that's explicitly out of scope (see `08-icp-positioning.md`, Enterprise section).

### Target metric
- None. Explicitly deferred — do not staff or measure against this channel until middle-market segment is revisited.
