# Customer Acquisition

This doc covers the specific acquisition channels Gekko will use to convert awareness into signups, layered on top of the distribution motions in `09-distribution.md`. The guiding constraint from CLAUDE.md and the neobank roadmap: acquisition should exploit the existing Suwappu Telegram/WhatsApp base first (near-zero CAC) before spending on channels that acquire net-new crypto-curious users.

## X / LinkedIn / Insta / TikTok

### What it is
Social platform presence and content distribution, each with different audience fit — X for crypto-native/trading audience and real-time discourse, LinkedIn for SMB/founder-to-founder credibility, Instagram/TikTok for broader consumer/short-form reach.

### Playbook / options
- X: primary channel for crypto-native audience — threads, build-in-public, yield transparency posts, engagement with trading/DeFi discourse.
- LinkedIn: founder posts targeting the SMB/startup/DAO-operator audience — treasury management, payroll-in-stablecoins angle.
- Instagram/TikTok: lower priority initially; short-form "your money should work for you" consumer-education content if/when broadening past crypto-native (verify audience fit before investing).
- Post cadence benchmark: 3-5x/week on primary channel (X) sustained beats daily-then-silent patterns.

### Gekko default
X is the primary channel, full stop, given the existing crypto-native audience overlap. LinkedIn is secondary and founder-led for the SMB/DAO wedge. Instagram/TikTok deferred until there's a broader-consumer product motion to support them.

### Target metric
- X follower-to-signup conversion: track click-through to Gekko onboarding link, target 2%+ (verify against channel norms).
- LinkedIn: number of inbound SMB/DAO conversations generated per month, target 5-10 by quarter 2.

## SEO / GEO

### What it is
Search engine optimization (ranking in Google/Bing results) and generative engine optimization (being cited/recommended by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) — both compounding, low-marginal-cost acquisition channels.

### Playbook / options
- SEO: target long-tail queries like "stablecoin savings account," "non-custodial debit card," "crypto neobank" — build comparison and educational content that ranks (verify current SERP competition).
- GEO: ensure Gekko has clear, structured, factual public content (docs, comparison pages, FAQ) that AI answer engines can cite when users ask "best non-custodial crypto banking app" type questions — structured data and clarity matter more than keyword density here.
- Content investment: comparison pages (Gekko vs. Gnosis Pay vs. Zeal vs. traditional bank), yield explainer pages, "how it works" docs.
- This is a multi-quarter compounding investment, not a fast channel — budget patience accordingly.

### Gekko default
Start GEO-first: publish clear, factual, well-structured public docs and comparison content early (cheap, compounds, and AI answer engines are increasingly where crypto-curious users start research) while treating traditional SEO as a slower-burn parallel track.

### Target metric
- Organic search + AI-referral signups as % of total: track from month 1, target 10%+ by end of year one (verify baseline once tracking is live).
- Number of indexed/ranking comparison and educational pages: target 15-20 published in first 2 quarters.

## Paid Acquisition

### What it is
Paid channels — search ads, social ads, crypto-native ad networks — used to acquire users directly rather than through owned/organic/referral channels.

### Playbook / options
- Crypto-native ad networks (e.g., placements on trading terminals, DeFi news sites) tend to outperform generic paid social for this audience (verify current CPM/CPA benchmarks — this space shifts fast).
- Standard fintech paid acquisition CAC benchmarks run $50-200+ per funded account depending on channel and targeting (verify — highly variable, use as a rough anchor only).
- Retargeting users who visited but didn't complete onboarding is typically the highest-ROI paid spend before broad top-of-funnel spend.
- Avoid broad paid social spend until organic/referral funnels are instrumented and converting — paid should scale a proven funnel, not substitute for one.

### Gekko default
Hold paid acquisition spend near zero for the first 1-2 quarters. The existing Suwappu base is enough top-of-funnel to validate onboarding/activation without paying for traffic. Reintroduce paid only for retargeting once funnel metrics are known, then consider crypto-native ad placements for scaling the SMB/DAO wedge.

### Target metric
- Paid CAC vs. LTV ratio: do not scale spend until LTV:CAC exceeds 3:1 (standard fintech benchmark, verify against Gekko's actual unit economics once known).
- % of total signups from paid: keep under 10% until quarter 3+.

## Events

### What it is
In-person or virtual conferences, meetups, and hackathons where Gekko can build relationships, demo the product, and recruit early users — particularly effective in crypto where trust is built face-to-face.

### Playbook / options
- Crypto conferences (major ones: ETHDenver, Token2049, Consensus — verify current relevant events and dates) for founder/BD presence and product demos.
- Local meetups in crypto hub cities for smaller-scale, higher-intimacy relationship building with DAOs/Web3 studios (the SMB vertical).
- Hackathon sponsorship as a way to reach developer-adjacent crypto-native users who influence broader adoption.
- Events are relationship/credibility plays, not high-volume acquisition — budget and measure accordingly.

### Gekko default
Selective, low-cost presence (booth or panel, not full sponsorship) at 2-3 major crypto events per year, prioritized for BD conversations with DAO/protocol treasury prospects over broad consumer lead-gen.

### Target metric
- Qualified SMB/DAO conversations generated per event: target 10-15 per major event attended.
- Not tracked as a primary consumer-signup channel.

## Partnerships

### What it is
Acquisition-focused collaborations distinct from the broader strategic partnerships in `09-distribution.md` — specifically co-marketing or integration deals designed to directly drive signups (joint campaigns, bundled offers, cross-promotion).

### Playbook / options
- Co-marketing with DeFi protocols Gekko integrates with (Aave, Morpho) — joint announcement/campaign when yield integrations go live.
- Cross-promotion with complementary (non-competing) crypto tools used by the same ICP.
- Bundled offers: e.g., a fee discount or yield boost for users of a partner wallet/tool who onboard to Gekko.

### Gekko default
Time partnership-driven acquisition campaigns to product milestones (e.g., yield feature launch, card launch) rather than running them as an always-on channel — this concentrates impact and reuses the credibility of the integration itself as the marketing hook.

### Target metric
- Signups per partnership campaign: track individually per campaign, no standing target until first campaign run.

## Influencers

### What it is
Paid or gifted promotion through crypto-native content creators (X/YouTube/TikTok) with audiences that overlap the target ICP — a channel with wide variance in quality and a history of trust issues in crypto specifically.

### Playbook / options
- Prioritize creators known for trading/DeFi education content over pure hype accounts — audience quality matters more than follower count.
- Structure deals with performance components (referral codes/links) rather than flat-fee-only, to measure real conversion.
- Disclosure/compliance: ensure all sponsored content follows applicable disclosure rules (verify current requirements for financial products specifically).
- Crypto influencer marketing has a credibility problem post-2022 cycle scandals — vet creators carefully; a bad partner is reputationally costly for a banking product specifically.

### Gekko default
Low priority initially, and if pursued, restricted to a small number of creators already trusted within the existing trading community Suwappu serves (i.e., people who already have organic reach into the same Telegram/Discord/X circles) rather than broad-reach general crypto influencers.

### Target metric
- If run: referral-code-attributed signups per campaign, with a minimum bar of positive ROI (signup value > campaign cost) before renewing any creator relationship.

## Warm Introductions

### What it is
Person-to-person introductions — from the team's network, existing users, advisors, or investors — that route new users or business accounts to Gekko through a trusted relationship rather than a channel.

### Playbook / options
- Systematically ask advisors/investors for 3-5 warm intros each to relevant SMB/DAO/startup prospects — a standard early-stage B2B tactic that's easy to under-invest in.
- Existing Suwappu power users and community moderators are a warm-intro asset for the SMB/DAO vertical — treat them as an informal referral network beyond the formal referral program.
- Track intro sources so the best-performing relationships can be doubled down on.

### Gekko default
Run this as an explicit, tracked motion for the SMB/DAO-treasury vertical specifically (see `08-icp-positioning.md`) — ask every advisor, investor, and known Suwappu community leader for warm intros before any account moves into a colder outbound motion.

### Target metric
- Number of warm intros converted to opened accounts per month: target 5+ by quarter 2 of the SMB motion.

## One-to-Many Distribution

### What it is
Any mechanism that reaches many potential users through a single action or piece of content — viral loops, in-product sharing, community-wide announcements in existing Telegram/WhatsApp groups — as opposed to one-to-one acquisition motions.

### Playbook / options
- In-bot broadcast: a single announcement to the existing Suwappu Telegram bot user base reaches the entire installed base at once — the highest-leverage channel Gekko has.
- Community-wide drops or promotions (e.g., "first 1,000 accounts get X") to create urgency and social sharing.
- Shareable, branded content (e.g., a "you earned $X in yield this month" card) designed to be screenshotted and shared organically.

### Gekko default
Treat the in-bot/in-channel broadcast to existing Suwappu users as the single highest-leverage acquisition action available and sequence the entire launch around it — everything else in this doc is secondary until that channel is fully exploited.

### Target metric
- Signup rate within 48 hours of a broadcast announcement: target 5-10% of the notified base (verify against actual bot audience size).
