# Gusto / Rippling payroll UX research — pass 1 (Nov 2026)

Pass 1 from search snippets + fetched pages (Gusto help center 403'd for WebFetch; Rippling help needs login). Pass 2 (browser) appended below when available.

## Parity requirements
- **Run-payroll wizard (Gusto):** Hours & Earnings → Time Off / Benefits / Deductions → Review and Submit. [merchantmaverick](https://www.merchantmaverick.com/how-to-use-gusto-payroll)
  - Review shows three summaries: "What Gets Taxed and Debited", "What Your Employees Worked & Take Home", "What Your Company Pays".
  - Hours & Earnings rows have an Actions menu (Edit Deductions).
  - Pre-submit alerts flag changes, never alter pay. [gusto alerts](https://support.gusto.com/article/260112145935220/review-changes-to-your-regular-payroll-with-helpful-alerts-for-admins)
- **Rippling pay run** compares against prior runs; drill into gross pay, tax, deduction changes. [rippling payroll](https://www.rippling.com/products/payroll)
- **Statuses (Gusto Embedded):** Unprocessed → Submitted → Pending → Paid; edit only Unprocessed; cancel only Submitted; debit 4pm PT on deadline; late submit slips a business day. [gusto docs](https://docs.gusto.com/embedded-payroll/docs/payroll-statuses)
- **Speeds (verified via browser):** standard Next-day, 2-day, 4-day; faster Same-day, Instant Pay (eligible payrolls and US contractor payments only). 2-day/4-day in all plans; next-day in Plus/Premium (add-on for Simple); Same-day/Instant included in Premium, paid add-on otherwise. [gusto speeds](https://support.gusto.com/article/999752211000000/Direct-deposit-payment-speeds-in-Gusto) Instant Pay can't be cancelled.
- **Off-cycle:** Bonus (skips regular deductions, supplemental tax rate), Correction (includes deductions, regular rate), Dismissal (state termination rules). [gusto off-cycle](https://support.gusto.com/article/999908231000000/Off-cycle-payrolls) Rippling: titled off-cycle runs, unlimited/free native; $50/employee/run some EOR/partner jurisdictions. [fitsmallbusiness](https://fitsmallbusiness.com/how-to-do-payroll-with-rippling/) [rippling review](https://www.rippling.com/blog/rippling-payroll-review)
- **Roles/approvals:** Gusto admins All access vs Custom; only account owner manages roles [gusto admins](https://support.gusto.com/article/114138050100000/Types-of-administrators); approver can Submit / Edit / Decline [gusto approvals](https://support.gusto.com/article/240829150046240/Set-up-approvals-for-payroll). Rippling permission profiles = scope × access × actions (view/edit/approve), assigned by attributes not people [rippling permissions](https://www.rippling.com/platform/permissions).
- **Reports:** Gusto standard + saved custom templates, PDF/CSV/XLSX; custom categories Premium [gusto reports](https://support.gusto.com/article/101334493100000/View-download-and-customize-reports-in-Gusto-for-admins). Rippling no-code builder with permission-scoped sharing.
- **Taxes & compliance (Gusto):** Tax setup, Tax documents, Report a notice, Agency payments report; filings failing 3× must be filed outside Gusto. [gusto notices](https://support.gusto.com/article/106621962100000/How-to-handle-a-tax-notice-from-an-agency-for-admins)
- **GL mapping (Rippling):** QBO/Xero/NetSuite mapped by department, location, job code (Rippling-authored review).
- **Sentiment:** G2 Gusto 4.6, Rippling 4.8; Gusto top complaint support; Rippling learning curve, Android app. [g2](https://www.g2.com/compare/gusto-vs-rippling) [merchantmaverick rippling](https://www.merchantmaverick.com/reviews/rippling-payroll-review)

## Pass 2 — Gusto, verified by headless browser (Nov 2026)
- **Help-center categories (proxy for app areas):** Company set up and details, Payroll, People, Reports, Tax credits, Taxes forms and compliance, Time & Attendance, App directory, Benefits. [support.gusto.com](https://support.gusto.com/) Payroll subcategories: Gusto Money, Blocked payroll, Payroll history, Paying your team (Processing payrolls, Paying contractors). [hub](https://support.gusto.com/hub/Employers-and-admins/Payroll)
- **Home / to-dos:** Notifications Center (bell, top nav, any page) with **Tasks** and **Activity** tabs; employees see Tasks + Recent activity on Home. [178361225624246](https://support.gusto.com/article/178361225624246) Approval requests appear as a task on admin Home. [240829150046240](https://support.gusto.com/article/240829150046240)
- **Run payroll:** spreadsheet view, mobile app, legacy view; upcoming payrolls listed in **Pay** with **run-by date** and **payday**; only admins with payroll-processing permission can run. [999754831000000](https://support.gusto.com/article/999754831000000) Payroll overview: payroll calendar, debit dates, funding requirements, wires, search/sort/filter/**bulk edit**, bank transaction codes. [186733245100000](https://support.gusto.com/article/186733245100000) Alerts only on regular runs, before submit. [260112145935220](https://support.gusto.com/article/260112145935220) Run types: off-cycle, dismissal, past check date. [processing](https://support.gusto.com/hub/Employers-and-admins/Payroll/Paying-your-team/Processing-payrolls) Cancel unprocessed; change or reverse processed; change/cancel contractor payment. [106621949100000](https://support.gusto.com/article/106621949100000)
- **Approvals:** regular, off-cycle, new hire, transition, 401(k) max-out, correction/extra pay/dismissal; not contractor, Canadian, reversal; free on all plans; "Request"/"Review" approval-only functions. [240829150046240](https://support.gusto.com/article/240829150046240)
- **Automations:** payroll preparation (auto-approve timesheets matching schedule, flag hour fixes, Pre-payroll review email); auto-submit on deadline day, 2:00 p.m. PT cutoff. [179037380821161](https://support.gusto.com/article/179037380821161) **Payroll on AutoPilot** runs one day before deadline, 24 h to change/cancel, all plans. [999755421000000](https://support.gusto.com/article/999755421000000)
- **Admin types:** All access admin (unlimited); Custom admin (Plus: 1, Premium: unlimited); People manager (unlimited); Outside collaborators (accountant, bookkeeper, other); payroll sub-permissions "edit payroll access" / "full payroll access". [114138050100000](https://support.gusto.com/article/114138050100000)
- **Standard reports:** Reports by department; Workforce costing; Workforce costing calculation (job costing); Labor cost allocation; Time off; Time-tracking hours; **Cash requirements**; Workers' comp audit; Pre-tax benefit account; Compensation change history. Reports in left menu, needs reporting permission; Pay > Pay History for payroll summary. [101334493100000](https://support.gusto.com/article/101334493100000)
- **Taxes & compliance:** Forms and taxes filed by Gusto; Worker's compensation; End of year; Compliance hub; state Business Compliance; tax notices; tax reconciliation payrolls. Tax credits: tip, EITC, R&D. [hub](https://support.gusto.com/hub/Employers-and-admins/Taxes-forms-and-compliance)
- **Contractors:** US by direct deposit or check (admin prints), no limit, Same-day/Instant eligible. [100739998100000](https://support.gusto.com/article/100739998100000) International: estimated delivery date per payment, automated + one-time payments, transfer purpose codes, cancel upcoming, Contractor payments report. [106622337100000](https://support.gusto.com/article/106622337100000)
- **Employee self-service:** paystubs on/after check date, Paycheck Comparison [260428150612093](https://support.gusto.com/article/260428150612093); multiple bank accounts with **split**, paper check, payroll cards. [999903851000000](https://support.gusto.com/article/999903851000000)
