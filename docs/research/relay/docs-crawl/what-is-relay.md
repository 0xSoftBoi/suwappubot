What is Relay?

Enterprise-grade onchain payments infrastructure

Relay is payments infrastructure that brings the convenience and confidence of fiat rails onchain. Integrate once to reach every supported chain, with execution enterprises can rely on and the tooling to build the experience your users expect.

Major Use Cases
Accept onchain payments
Let customers pay from supported chains and settle in the asset your application uses.
Move assets across chains
Bridge assets between supported chains through one transaction flow.
Add swaps
Exchange assets on the same chain or across different chains.
Execute cross-chain calls
Let users take an action on one chain and pay with assets from another.
