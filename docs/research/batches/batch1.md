# Batch 1 — Winners 1–3

## 1. Zauth (@zauthx402) — winner #1

**What it does:** Trust/security infrastructure for the "agentic economy," built around the x402 micropayment protocol (HTTP 402 revived for AI agent payments). Two named products: **Vector** ($20 AI-powered pentesting/vulnerability scanner, OWASP Top 10 coverage with real-browser exploit verification) and **RepoScan** (repo risk-scoring, distributed via integrations with crypto terminals Axiom, GMGN, and Terminal). Runs a "Provider Hub" for x402 endpoint monitoring; claims 30%+ of x402 endpoints tested were broken. [gritdaily](https://gritdaily.com/zauth-building-the-trust-layer-ai-desperately-needs/), [zauthx402.com](https://zauthx402.com/verification), [zauth.inc](https://zauth.inc/)

**Team:** Solo-founder narrative — "Dylan" (surname not disclosed in any source found). Started trading in a college dorm 2021 with $500; cites FTX collapse/rug pulls as motivation. No co-founders or team roster surfaced. UNVERIFIED: full identity/LinkedIn.

**Token & market:** $ZAUTH on Solana, CA `DNhQZ1CE9qZ2FNrVhsCXwQJ2vZG8ufZkcYakTS5Jpump`. ATH $0.007078 (Jan 28 2026, right after the win) — now ~-76% off ATH, ~$0.0017–0.0044 depending on source, mcap ~$1.5–1.8M. [dexcheck](https://dexcheck.ai/app/solana/chart/CJbrDKgS5Vi3i7Re2Tt8bKttYhBUdC5NfgL18iNptodX), [coingecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/zauthx402)

**Traction:** RepoScan claims 90,000+ users "almost overnight" via B2B distribution deals with Axiom, GMGN, Terminal (real, known Solana trading terminals — credible signal if accurate). No independent verification of the 90k figure.

**Funding:** $250k from Pump Fund at $10M valuation. No other round reported.

**Links:** zauth.inc, zauthx402.com, X @zauthx402. No public GitHub found.

**Skeptical read:** Most substantive of the three on product evidence — live scanner product, stated price, documented third-party integrations, specific technical claims. But: pseudonymous founder, token -76% from ATH within ~7 months (hackathon-pump-then-dump pattern), 90k user number self-reported. Real shipping B2B security tool riding a memecoin-funded go-to-market; the token chart already looks like sell-the-news.

## 3. Pumpcade (@pumpcade; founder Pop Punk, @PopPunkOnChain) — winner #3

**What it does:** Real-time, ultra-short-duration prediction markets (60 seconds–30 minutes resolution windows) designed to embed into livestreams and browser contexts. A browser extension lets users create markets on pages with verifiable data sources (sports scores, streams, news). Core pitch: "automatic, deterministic, verifiable" resolution via direct official API calls or math, avoiding the oracle disputes that plague Polymarket-style markets. Docs at docs.cade.market. As of the $5M raise (mid-April 2026), the app was reported still in testing / "open beta coming soon." [cryptopolitan](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/pump-fun-completes-5m-funding-for-pumpcade/), [docs.cade.market](https://docs.cade.market/usage/placing-predictions)

**Team:** Founder is pseudonymous crypto-Twitter figure "Pop Punk" (@PopPunkOnChain), an established Solana-native builder persona; real identity not disclosed. No other named team members surfaced.

**Token & market:** $PUMPCADE on Solana, CA `Eg2ymQ2aQqjMcibnmTt8erC6Tvk9PVpJZCxvVPJz2agu`. ATH $0.04804 on April 15 2026 (around the $5M seed announcement) — now down ~71% from ATH; mcap estimates vary widely, roughly $14–30M by snapshot (DexScreener ~$14.47M). 118K+ token holders reported. [dexscreener](https://dexscreener.com/solana/fkfrk3wdagxokysr1fpggcdeegkv1b1yynx9ykvpg3jg), [coingecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/pumpcade)

**Traction:** 118K+ on-chain holders (distribution signal for a token, not proof of product usage). No independently verified product usage metrics (trades placed, markets resolved, DAU) found.

**Funding — VERIFIED:** Pop Punk's claim of "$6M across 2 rounds in 12 days" checks out: **$1M pre-seed led by Pump.fun** (~Apr 2 2026) + **$5M seed co-led by Jump Crypto and Foundation Capital** (~Apr 14 2026) = $6M, implied ~$44M valuation. Corroborated independently by Bitget, Cryptopolitan, Blockster, Tekedia, PANews. He also claims he hired the person who ran the Pump.fun hackathon. [tekedia](https://www.tekedia.com/pumpcade-announces-an-oversubscribed-1m-pre-seed-round-led-by-pump-fun/), [panewslab](https://www.panewslab.com/en/articles/019d9924-a828-73ea-a377-6c90db2a3e93)

**Links:** X @pumpcade, @PopPunkOnChain, docs.cade.market. No GitHub found.

**Skeptical read:** Strongest-verified on the money side, with tier-1 crypto VCs named on the record. But product was still "beta coming soon" at the raise, no independent usage data, pseudonymous founder, closed-source, and token -71% from post-raise ATH — a hype-then-fade pattern consistent with a $44M valuation ahead of shipped product. Institutional conviction in the founder is not the same as verified PMF.
